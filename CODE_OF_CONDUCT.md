# Community Code of Conduct

We want AI Wedding Photography Pro to be a professional, welcoming, evidence-driven open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas, prompts, tests, and implementations—not people.
- Clearly distinguish verified platform behavior from assumptions.
- Respect privacy, consent, licensing, and cultural context.
- Avoid demeaning language about body type, disability, age, appearance, gender expression, or personal features.
- Give photographers, stylists, developers, testers, and non-code contributors equal respect.
- Prefer reproducible evidence over authority or brand preference.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private client images or sensitive personal information without permission.
- Submitting copyrighted, proprietary, or restricted assets without redistribution rights.
- Deliberately misrepresenting model/runtime capabilities or test results.
- Using community spaces to pressure people into appearance changes or body modifications they did not request.

## Maintainer actions

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation in serious or repeated cases.

If you experience a problem, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub with enough context to review it safely.
